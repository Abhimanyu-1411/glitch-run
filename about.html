ABOUT GLITCH RUN

Escape the simulation.

The game

GLITCH RUN is a browser-based neon cyberpunk endless runner. Run through a procedurally generated world, avoid triangle-spike obstacles, collect glowing data fragments, and see how long you can survive. Distance contributes to your score; collected fragments earn in-game coins.

What you can do

  • Collect shield, slow-motion, and data-magnet power-ups.
  • Survive changing glitch events, including Data Storm, Speed Surge, Gravity Error, and Corrupt Display.
  • Spend in-game coins on cosmetic skins and trails.
  • Work toward achievements and three rotating daily missions.
  • Adjust sound effects, music, screen shake, and reduced-motion settings.

Controls

  • Jump: Space, W, or Up Arrow. On touchscreens, tap.
  • Fast drop: Down Arrow or S while airborne. On mobile, swipe down or use the drop button.
  • Pause: P or Escape.

Game progress and settings are saved in this browser. Daily missions follow the device’s local date.
